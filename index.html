About Us

Our story begins in 1998 in a small office in Deira, Dubai. Mr. Girish Bhagtani had a vision of giving Dubai the kind of household, electric and electronics business it deserved. A business that would not compromise on quality and standards. He also expanded the business to offer services in food , non-food and personal care items.

Sky Colors established in 2017, specializes in the wholesale distribution of food, non-food, and personal care products. With over 9 years of experience, we have expanded globally, building long-standing relationships with suppliers and customers alike.
